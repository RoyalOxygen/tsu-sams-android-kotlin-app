What's inside

Built for every TSU classroom.

One platform for students, lecturers, and administrators — from lecture attendance to exam biometric capture.

Facial biometric attendance

Students mark attendance with a face scan. The system matches biometrics against the enrolled record in under two seconds.

Geofenced capture

Attendance only counts when a student is physically inside the lecture venue. Location is validated against the course geofence.

Real-time dashboards

Lecturers and admins see attendance live. Daily, weekly, and course-level summaries with no manual collation.

Offline-resilient

Weak network in the hall? The app shows a clear offline state and retries automatically the moment you're back online.

Role-based access

Students, lecturers, admins, and helpdesk each get tailored views. Every action is tied to the correct account and permissions.

University-grade security

HTTPS end-to-end, scoped camera access, and controlled biometric storage on TSU infrastructure. No third-party tracking.

Questions

Answers before you enroll.

Everything students ask about TSU SAMS.

Is the app free for TSU students?

Yes. TSU SAMS is free to download and use for all Taraba State University students. No hidden fees, ever.

Do I need to be on campus to mark attendance?

Yes. Attendance is geofenced — you must be physically inside the lecture venue when you scan. This prevents proxy attendance and keeps records accurate.

What is the difference between SAMS and Exam Capture?

SAMS handles lecture attendance with facial biometrics and geofencing. Exam Capture is the exam biometric companion — enroll your face once and verify instantly at exam entry. Both share your student verification.

What happens if I have no internet in the lecture hall?

The app detects the offline state instantly and shows a clear message. When connectivity returns it retries automatically. Your enrolled biometric remains valid.

Can I use the app on iPhone?

The native app is Android-only for now. iPhone users can access the SAMS web portal at sams.tsuniversity.edu.ng using Safari.

Who do I contact for help?

Visit the ICT Directorate at sams.tsuniversity.edu.ng or use the Helpdesk from the portal. TSU SAMS is Secured by Taraba State University ICT Directorate.
